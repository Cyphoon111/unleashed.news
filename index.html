How we beat the world to the story

Detect. Verify. Publish first.

  1. 01

    Detect Fire Alert

    Our Fire Alert system monitors keywords, phrases, handles and hashtags across social media and measures how fast each post is being shared, minute by minute. On X that's reposts per minute (RPM). The moment a post crosses the threshold, it's on a trajectory to become a viral event, and we know it first.

  2. 02

    Verify No source, no story

    Every developing event is investigated before a word is written. Every claim we make is backed by a primary source (CAT 1), or validated by a CAT 2 or CAT 3 source. Nothing publishes on a rumor. Not once, not ever.

  3. 03

    Publish First, and bulletproof

    Comprehensive, sourced stories go out programmatically, before the rest of the news networks can catch on and before the rest of the world starts to read about it. Investigative reports built to survive any challenge.

The sourcing standard

Three categories. One rule.

CAT 1

Primary source

The document, the footage, the data, the person who was there. First-hand and in the open.

CAT 2

Direct confirmation

An on-the-record confirmation from a party with direct knowledge, validating the claim.

CAT 3

Independent corroboration

Separate, verifiable reporting that corroborates the claim on its own.

The rule: no claim is published without a CAT 1 source, or a CAT 2 or CAT 3 source validating it. The mainstream doesn't practice that religiously. We do.
